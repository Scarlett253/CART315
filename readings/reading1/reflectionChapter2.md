#Reflective Writing: The Stuff Games Are Made Of - Chapter 2
During this chapter, Pippin guide us throught an experience he had when he asked himself the "What If" about the videogame Pong. He started coming out with many ideas on how to modify the game and get different variations of it, as examples: Blind Pong, Ghost Pong, Unfair Pong, and my favourites, Viennese Pong and Fertility Pong. He talks about how a silly thought can be transform into a magnificent visual artwork, getting deeper and deeper with each explanation of each of the variations.

What I liked about this chapter is that Pippin's writing style and his way to tell the readers about his experiences, feels genuine and not just like a self insert. He explains it in a way I can feel connected with his ideas and share the same emotions on the outcome of each of his projects.

As one of Pippin's old students, I remember doing the activity he mentioned in the chapter, about adding and changing the rules of an existing game, as a result, many of us got just variables of the same game, while others, went to far and created a whole new different game with small similitudes of the original idea.

This chapter feels like a wise advice in how to improve the creativity when it comes into creating a videogame. I like the idea he gives about how we should always make ourselves questions about the projects we are working on even if we are about to finish it, to use the "What If" to fullfill our wonder and curiosity.

This chapter teach me how small ideas can become big changes if I used them in the correct way, and it also teach me about how mistakes are not necessarly bad outcomes, but can create masterpieces.
