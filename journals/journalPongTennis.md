#Pong Prototype: Friendly Tennis

25/sep
Initial idea came to me during work since I work in  tennis club as receptionist.



While Creating:

-Changed code controls of P1 and P2 because I wanted to use WASD and all four arrows to give more freedom of movement so it can actually look like tennis. Althought I had to change it  many times while looking for tutorials for multiplayer.

-While coding and testing, noticed the ball would bug and get into an x loop direction left to right every time it was hit by the paddles --> fix it and modified in a way that everytime it gets hit, its not perfect and added a spin effect to make it more realistic.

-100% accurate tennis court

-When move back and foward, the hit is more intense and increase the ball's speed --> not intentional = "HAPPY ACCIDENT"
