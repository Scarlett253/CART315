# Walking simulator - 3D Prototype

26/Oct

My first idea was to make either an aquatic environment inspired in the planet Miller from the movie Interestellar. there would be and infinite space of just water with relaxing wave sounds and watery steps.

My second idea was to make a desert inspired in the border between Mexico and United States. I was watching Breaking Bad during that week so I wanted to add as well the RV of the first season and some blue crystals somewhere on the place.

At the end I couldn't do any of these ideas because of the short knowledge I have of Unity and the 3D modeling, although I would love to recreate them in a coming future.

27/Oct

After some reflection, I opted to do a different environment of my two ideas, I wanted to create a world of just snow.

I downloaded different 3D packages from Unity assets of snow textures and tried them all to see which one fitted better the idea I had in mind. 

I tried following some youtube tutorials and did my best.

28/Oct

After a long proccess of making everything snow, I noticed the place was kind of empty besides the robots of the original template that I decided to kept. 

A new idea came to my mind, I would add a snowman that throw me snowballs instead of the robots.

I changed my mind pretty quick because I didn't wanted just to do a christmas variation of the template so I coded the snowman to be just randomly appearing.

It didn'worked well, but then it gave me a new idea, I would make him teleport, appear and disappear randomly when I looked at it.

Didn't love it because it looked like a glitch instead of being creepy.

29/Oct

I changed it again and now the purpose of the snowman was to follow me when I was not looking at it.

I loved it and I stuck at my idea and tried different ways on unity to reach that goal.

I failed a lot but at the end what worked was to use the same actions of the robots but with different configuration.

Now the snowman was being creepy, appearing and disappearing, and at the same time it would come closer and closer everytime I don't look at him.
