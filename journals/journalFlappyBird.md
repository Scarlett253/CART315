# Flappy Bird Prototype: Bambi Flaps

10/oct
The idea came to me watching my chihuahua Bambi. She's terrified of the vacuum cleaner and the broom. the moment she sees them, she runs to hide. Since she used to get into the kitchen a lot whenever I was cooking, I thought: what if that's the world? A kitchen where Bambi has to dodge everything that scares her.

While Creating:

-Started the project on 13/oct. First I made Bambi the main object (rigid body / bird) so she moves like the original bird.

-On 14/oct I added the obstacles and the obstacle spawner. It was buggy, it didn't work right. Kept debugging it that same day.

-On 17/oct I finally fixed it so the obstacles spawn, move to the left and disappear in the deadZone. Now time to fix the random Y positions.

-That same 17/oct I set up the open-and-close motion of the obstacles (like the pipes that open and close). Almost got it, still needed debugging.

-On 18/oct I added a background. Hadn't fixed the bug yet, but the background was there. That was it for that day.

-On 19/oct I kept debugging the obstacles that open and close. The score and game over were the hardest part of all, I was fighting with that.

-On 20/oct FINALLY fixed it: no more duplication of objects and they move correctly now. Now on to the scoring system.

-That same 20/oct I was finally able to create the score system, the game over and the start button. And to wrap it up: FINISH!!!!!! Added the sounds and it's done.
